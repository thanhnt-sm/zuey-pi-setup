## Anti-Pattern Guards (Mode 47 fires automatically)

- **Over-utilization (> 100%):** Block sprint lock; PM must rebalance OR explicit override with justification
- **Under-utilization (< 80%):** Warn (not block); suggest add filler P1-P2 OR offer DEV as backup
- **Senior idle (Senior with < 70% utilization while Junior > 95%):** Warn — likely poor assignment
